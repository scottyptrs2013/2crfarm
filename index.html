Welcome to Our Ranch

At 2nd Chance Ranch, we're keeping things simple: raise good animals, make good products, and treat people right.

From our farm to your table.

What started with a little land and a whole lot of stubbornness has grown into a working family farm offering fresh eggs and our own handcrafted pickled products.

  • Fresh farm eggs
  • Small-batch pickles & pickled eggs
  • Products made with pride right here at the ranch
  • Veteran owned and operated

Why “2nd Chance”?

Because sometimes life gives you another shot — and you damn sure ought to make something good out of it.

That's what this ranch is about.
